# Code of Conduct

## Standard

Everyone participating in OpenRun should be able to contribute without harassment. Be respectful, precise and constructive. Critique code and ideas; do not attack people. Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience. Respect privacy and attribution.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised conduct, doxxing and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Do not publish private conversations, contact details, routes or health data without consent.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces, and when someone represents the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through a private contact method the owner publishes on their GitHub profile. No dedicated confidential conduct inbox is configured yet; do not post sensitive details in an issue. If no private channel is available, ask the owner to establish one without naming affected people publicly.

Maintainers review reports fairly and confidentially where possible, hear relevant parties and may remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily or ban repeated/severe misconduct. Maintainers involved in a complaint should not decide it themselves. An appeal can request review by another uninvolved maintainer when one is available.

The policy applies equally to contributors and maintainers. A dedicated reporting channel and additional independent moderators are roadmap items.
