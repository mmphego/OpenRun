# OpenRun store listing draft — not published

App name: OpenRun: Run Around Your Life

Short description: Running plans, GPS and coaching, with room for cycling, gym and recovery.

## Full description

Make room for running without losing sight of the rest of your training.

OpenRun brings your running plan and additional cycling, gym, hiking and mobility sessions into one weekly view. See schedule overlaps and review the spacing of demanding sessions. You remain in control: scheduling suggestions do not silently change your running mileage.

Build a plan around your current running, preview it before saving, and follow warm-up, main-set and cool-down steps. Returning runners can choose run/walk sessions. Record outdoor runs with GPS and optional spoken workout cues, then reflect on effort, fatigue and pain.

Explore 18 original offline coaching guides, strength and mobility routines, recorded routes, weekly trends and GPS-derived distance estimates. Add your next workout to the home screen, pause or resume from the recording notification, and save an activity card that excludes your route and location.

Core features work without an account. Optional Health Connect access is controlled separately. Export a local backup to keep a copy of your training history.

Important limits: coaching uses generated editorial content and original rules, with no qualified coach review or medical assessment. GPS estimates are not certified results. Health Connect integration requires Android 14 or later. Garmin automatic account sync is not active; supported running files can be imported manually. Optional cloud and Strava features require a separately configured OpenRun service and provider credentials. OpenRun is independent of Runna and Garmin.
