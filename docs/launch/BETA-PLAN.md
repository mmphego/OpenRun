# Four-week beta — prepared, not executed

Audience hypothesis: adults who want a running plan that fits around cycling, gym and an inconsistent week. Test this proposition before broad acquisition spending. Pricing is undecided; no subscription or payment gate is implemented.

Recruit 10–20 consenting adult runners with varied experience and Android devices, including a Samsung S23/Android 16, an API 26–29 device, a Pixel/current Android, and a lower-memory phone. Recruit externally only after owner approval. No testers have been contacted by this release task.

Week 1: fresh install, onboarding timing, first-plan understanding, recording permission denial/approval, first workout and feedback. Observe where assistance is needed rather than setting arbitrary success claims.

Week 2: screen-off outdoor sessions, music and calls, notification pause/resume, offline use and interruption recovery. Never deliberately force-stop a participant's only copy of an important run; use dedicated test recordings.

Week 3: running plus cycling/gym calendar, overlap warnings, rescheduling, pain holds, backup restore on test data, Health Connect revoke/regrant and repeated imports. Garmin automatic syncing is not a testable feature without partner access.

Week 4: repeat use, widget usefulness, accessibility at 1.0/1.3/2.0 font scales, image export/share, and whether runners would choose OpenRun again. Interview dropouts as well as active users.

Measure: onboarding completion, first recorded workout, weekly repeat workouts, reported lost recordings, battery impact, permission abandonment and support issues. Optional local event counts are descriptive per installation, not a population analytics backend. They are off by default, limited to 90 days, erased on opt-out, excluded from backups and shared only manually. Use explicit research consent and a secure research process before collecting participants' reports. Do not pool personal training data by default.

Release gates: no unresolved lost-run or data-loss defect; acceptable GPS/time comparison against a reference device; background lifecycle and power test evidence; screen-reader and large-font checks; qualified training review; privacy and Play declarations; production signing and account approval. Numeric battery/GPS/retention targets should be set from observed baseline and product requirements, not invented as achieved results.
